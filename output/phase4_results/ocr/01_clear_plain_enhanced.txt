System Design Specification

‘SmartScan Image Processing Evaluation Document

‘Computer vision systems frequently rely on document scanning pipelines to digitize printed
media. Real-world capture introduces geometric perspective distortion, uneven ambient
lighting, and sensor noise.

The automated pipeline detects the paper boundary using Canny edge filtering followed by
polygonal contour approximation. Once four comer vertices are identified, an inverse
homography perspective warp projects the document back to a frontal, flat plane.

‘Subsequent stages implement morphological background estimation to eliminate shadows
without washing out ink strokes. Finally, adaptive thresholding or Otsu binarization yields
optimal contrast for OCR analysis.
