Final Project Report — Part 1: Overview

‘SmartScan image Processing Evaluation Document

Introduction: The Smart Document Scanner project presents a real-time computer vision
workflow capable of converting unconstrained mobile phone captures into high-resolution
‘scanner quality outputs.

Motivation: Handheld captures suffer from perspective keystone distortion, non-uniform
ambient illumination, and motion blur. Traditional flatbed scanners eliminate these physical
anomalies through controlled optical geometry.

Objectives: 1. Accurate quadrangular paper contour localization. 2. Inverse projective
homography mapping. 3. Morphological illumination compensation. 4. High-accuracy OCR
transcription
